The Top 6 Scariest Horror Movies

These are the movies that have left the most lasting impact on the audience.

  1. 06

    Number six: The Texas Chainsaw Massacre

    The Texas Chainsaw Massacre is a classic horror film that introduced the iconic character Leatherface and set a new standard for the genre.

  2. 05

    Number five: Sinister

    Sinister is a chilling horror film that follows a true-crime writer who discovers a series of disturbing home movies, leading to a terrifying investigation.

  3. 04

    Number four: The Shining

    The Shining is a psychological horror film that follows a family as they become trapped in an isolated hotel, where supernatural forces begin to influence the mothers sanity.

  4. 03

    Number three: The Conjuring

    The Conjuring is a supernatural horror film that follows paranormal investigators as they confront a malevolent entity haunting a family in their home.

  5. 02

    Number two: Hereditary

    Hereditary is a psychological horror film that explores the dark legacy of a family, revealing terrifying secrets and supernatural occurrences that lead to a tragic outcome.

  6. 01

    The scariest one

    Number one: The Exorcist

    The Exorcist is a landmark horror film that depicts the terrifying experience of a young girl possessed by a demonic entity, and the efforts of two priests to save her.

3 Honarble Mentions

The Ring

The Ring is a horror film that follows a journalist as she investigates a cursed videotape that causes anyone who watches it to die within seven days.

Silence of the Lambs

Silence of the Lambs is a psychological horror film that follows an FBI trainee as she seeks the help of a canninalistic serial killer to catch another serial killer who skins his victims.

IT (2017)

IT (2017) is a horror film based on Stephen King's novel, following a group of teens as they confront a shape-shifting entity of a clown and terrorizes their town.
